# Product vision

Status: Draft

## Purpose

Indie Football provides a reliable place for grassroots football communities to
manage teams, players, tournaments, fixtures, and match results.

## Primary users

- Players who participate in teams and may link an account to a player profile.
- Team owners and managers who maintain staff, rosters, and match submissions.
- Tournament administrators who organize competitions and resolve result disputes.
- System administrators who manage platform-wide access and exceptional cases.

## Product principles

- Match results must be trustworthy and auditable.
- Team-specific authority must be explicit.
- A player profile may exist before its player creates an account.
- Destructive or corrective administrative actions must retain history.
- Rules should be enforced consistently in policies, services, and automated tests.

## Initial success criteria

- A user can create and manage a team without administrative intervention.
- A team can maintain a clear roster and staff structure.
- A tournament can enroll teams and schedule matches.
- Opposing teams can submit results and disagreements are surfaced for resolution.
- Published results and later corrections have a visible audit trail.

## Open questions

- Is the first release intended for one football community or multiple communities?
- Will the application support public browsing without an account?
- Are tournament administrators appointed globally or per tournament?
- Which notifications are required for the MVP?
