# MVP scope

Status: Draft

## Included

- Account registration, authentication, password recovery, and email verification.
- Global administrative roles and permissions.
- Team creation and team owner/manager administration.
- Player profiles and team roster membership.
- Player-to-user account linking.
- Team invitations.
- Tournament creation and team enrollment.
- Match scheduling.
- Result submission by participating teams.
- Automatic publication of matching submissions.
- Dispute handling for conflicting submissions.
- Administrative result corrections with an audit trail.

## Explicitly excluded from the MVP

- Payments, subscriptions, and tournament entry fees.
- Live match commentary or live score updates.
- Advanced player statistics and performance analytics.
- Native mobile applications.
- Transfers involving financial contracts.
- Public APIs for third-party integrations.

Items may move into or out of scope only through an accepted product decision. When
that happens, update this document and the applicable release plan together.
